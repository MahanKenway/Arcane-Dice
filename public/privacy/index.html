Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026. Arcane Dice is a browser-based dice roller maintained by Mahan Tavakoli.

What is stored

Roll history, selected dice, background choices, tint, volume, custom background images, and custom audio tracks are stored locally in your browser using local storage. They are not sent to an Arcane Dice account or first-party database. Clear site data in your browser to remove them.

Files and public requests

When you choose a custom image or audio track, the browser reads it locally and keeps the resulting data in local storage. The app requests public JavaScript, dice themes, fonts, and game assets needed for the interface. Those providers may process ordinary technical request data under their own policies.

Retention

Arcane Dice has no required login and no first-party analytics or advertising profile. Local preferences remain until you delete them or storage is cleared. External providers control their own logs and retention.

Third-party links

Links, fonts, and hosted libraries may be controlled by third parties. Review their policies before using external services. The project does not sell personal information.
